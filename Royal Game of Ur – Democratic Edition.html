How it works. Race all 7 pieces round the board and off with an exact roll. Land on ✺ for an extra turn; the centre ✺ is safe, and elsewhere in the shared lane you capture by landing on an enemy. Democracy: each citizen piece belongs to a faction and votes on every move: 🗡 Hawks back captures, 🛡 Guardians back rosettes and safe squares, 🏃 Runners back pushing the leader. As Speaker you add 2 votes (⭐) to one move, but the people can overrule you. The most votes wins, ties go to the Speaker. The losing side may then spend one of its 2 vetoes to throw out the winning move, and the runner-up plays instead. With only one legal move there is no vote. Referendums: every third round of yours, a law is put to the vote. Every citizen on both sides votes Aye or Nay by faction, and each Speaker adds 2 votes. A law stays in force until a later vote repeals it.
